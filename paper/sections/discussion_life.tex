In our model, a fleet that serves a continuous classifier spends as much energy on inference as it spent on training within 12 minutes to 1.7 days, and over a month 18 to 3\,400 times as much (Table~\ref{tab:life}). Two consequences follow. A federated training controller should be judged not only by the Joules it saves during training---which in absolute terms are small---but by whether it produces models that can be served cheaply: EcoFed's shallow exits are accurate enough that 70--97\% of windows leave at the first block on the wearable datasets, while the exits of a model trained with plain multi-exit FedAvg are not (23--70\%). And the training cost of making a model multi-exit is repaid within minutes to hours of operation, so there is no energy argument for deploying a single-exit network, even where single-exit training reaches a slightly higher accuracy (UCI HAR). On Speech Commands the deployment saving is small, because the task needs the deeper exits.
