\subsection{Pilot on exit weighting}
In a pilot on tuning seeds (UCI HAR, FedAvg, 4 seeds) uniform exit weights gave a validation accuracy at the end of the budget of 81.9\% at $\phi=0.1$ and 87.5\% at $\phi=1$, whereas weights proportional to $j^2$ gave 85.1\% and 88.5\%, and a single-exit objective gave 86.4\% and 88.6\%; the single-exit model was clearly worse early in the run (68.9\% against 79.6\% at 25\% of the capacity for the uniform weighting at $\phi=0.1$). We therefore use $\lambda_j\propto j^2$ for all methods. Hyper-parameters were tuned after this decision.

\subsection{Metered-hardware protocol (provided, not executed)}\label{app:hardware}
The study has no power measurements. To make the missing step cheap to carry out, the repository contains a harness that turns a metered device into a \texttt{DeviceProfile}: \texttt{energy/measure\_hw.py} runs local training for every depth and for batch sizes 16, 32 and 64 on the device it executes on, brackets each window with timestamps, and integrates the power above the idle level from one of three sources (an external meter log such as Joulescope or Nordic PPK2, Jetson \texttt{tegrastats}, or an INA219 on a Raspberry Pi supply line); \texttt{energy/fit\_profile.py} fits $\alpha_u$, $\beta_u$ and a fixed per-sample overhead $c_0$ by non-negative least squares to $E=\alpha\cdot3\,\mathrm{MAC}_{1:d}+\beta\,\mathrm{Bytes}_{1:d}(b)+c_0$ and reports $R^2$ and the mean absolute percentage error; radio energies per bit are obtained from timed transfers of files of known size over the real link. Setting the environment variable \texttt{ECOFED\_DEVICE\_JSON} replaces the assumed constants of Table~\ref{tab:devices} by the fitted ones in every experiment. The fitting code is covered by unit tests that recover known parameters from synthetic data (including with 5\% noise); the three power-source readers are untested because the required hardware was not available, and no number in this paper depends on them.
